# COMPANY PULSE — Century Communities (CCS) | FY25 3Q (10/22/2025)

## Latest-Quarter Read (3Q25): Demand is softer, but execution is buffering margins
3Q25 read-through is a “muted demand / higher incentive” tape, offset by tangible cost relief and faster cycle times. Management is effectively buying pace to protect throughput and year-end closings, while keeping spec inventory “right-sized” to avoid a glut.

- **Demand/pace:** Net new contracts fell sequentially (**-6%**) to 2,386, though management framed it as better than normal seasonality: *“net new contracts of 2,386 homes declined by 6% on a sequential basis”* and noted it compared favorably to historical seasonal declines (per summary).  
- **Demand quality signal:** They repeatedly attribute softness to the buyer psyche (confidence/affordability), especially entry-level: *“Homebuyer demand has been more muted this year due to weaker consumer confidence”* and *“entry level consumer has been the hardest hit.”*
- **Lead indicators mixed:** Web traffic improved (June–Sept), and early October orders were “seasonally consistent,” suggesting traffic ≠ conversion clarity: *“We saw month-over-month increase in our web traffic from June to September”* and *“Oct orders seasonally consistent with Aug/Sep”* (summary).
