## TPH — Company Pulse (FY25 3Q, call date 2025-10-23)

### Core Read (latest quarter anchored)
3Q25 was a **“stable-but-soft”** demand quarter where TPH protected pricing largely via **higher incentives (especially on specs)**, and is already guiding to a **4Q margin step-down** as that incentive/spec dynamic persists. Management’s tone suggests they see the environment as competitive and supply-driven in several key markets, not as a company-specific execution issue: “**Market conditions remained soft throughout the third quarter.
