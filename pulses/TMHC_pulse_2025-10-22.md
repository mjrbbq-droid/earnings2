## Margins & Incentives Mechanics (be specific)
**Q3 margin held up better than feared on mix (more TBB closings), but Q4 margin guidance steps down on spec closing penetration + incentive intensity.** Incentives are increasingly financial-services-driven and are used to “manufacture affordability” as rates stay high.

**1) What supported Q3 margin**
- Management points to mix benefit: “**The upside was due in part to a favorable mix of higher margin to-be-built home closings**.”  
- Cycle time improvements support close timing flexibility (helps push/pull mix): “**In the third quarter, we realized another roughly 10 days of sequential savings...about 30 days faster than a year ago**.”

**2) Why margins are set to roll over**
- Explicit mix headwind into Q4: “**We expect a modest mix headwind from a higher penetration of spec home closings**.”  
- Q4 guide frames the outcome: “**We expect...gross margin...to be approximately 21.5% in the fourth quarter**.”  
- Underlying reason: higher spec closings + competitive market requiring sharper pricing/incentives: “**We have adjusted pricing and incentives, particularly in entry-level price points**.”

**3) Incentive composition & “buydown” mechanics**
- Incentives are materially tied to financing tools: “**Somewhere around 45% of our incentives are specific to financial services**.”  
- Management is leaning on multiple structures (not just price cuts): “**We continue to use...buy-downs...adjustable loans...proprietary loans...to stimulate that business**.”  
- New structure to reduce fallout/extend lock: “**Introduced a new proprietary nine-month program for our to-be-builts...flexibility on a forward lock...free float-down**.”  
- Practical implication: **Specs require heavier incentive dollars to move**, while TBB can be supported with structured financing/lock tools. This echoes Q2 commentary that incentives become more expensive as inventory gets more complete (Q3 doesn’t restate it as bluntly, but the behavior is consistent).

**Direction vs prior quarters:** Q1 guided incentive step-up; Q2 said incentives would increase to clear inventory and margins would moderate; Q3 confirms **ongoing incentive adjustment**, with **Q4 margin guide dropping to ~21.5%** as spec closings rise.
