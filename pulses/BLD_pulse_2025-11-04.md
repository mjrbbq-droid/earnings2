# BLD — Company Pulse (FY25 Q3 focused)

## Latest Quarter Snapshot (FY25 Q3: 2025-11-04)
BLD’s Q3 read-through is a tale of **residential pricing pressure and softer demand** being **partly counterweighted by cost actions and stronger C&I backlogs**. Profitability held up better than the volume/pricing setup suggests, but margins are still drifting lower YoY and management is explicitly preparing investors for a **slightly worse price/cost headwind in Q4**.

- Profitability softer YoY: **“Adjusted EBITDA margin of 19.8%, down 100 basis points versus the third quarter of last year.”** (Kuhns) and **“Adjusted gross profit…30.1%, which compares to 30.7% last year.”** (Kuhns)  
- Demand tone weakened into Q3/Q4: **“It definitely got a little bit worse in Q3… Q4 is probably a little softer as well.”** (Kuhns)  
- Backlogs improving in several non-res channels: **“Backlogs across mechanical, DI and SPI, those are growing for sure.”** (Buck) and **“Commercial roofing… those backlogs are building as well…”** (Buck)  
- Guidance up (but largely acquisition-driven): **“We are raising our adjusted EBITDA guidance for the year to be between $1.01 billion to $1.06 billion.”** (Kuhns)

**Directional change vs last 4 quarters:** The operating narrative has moved from Q1–Q2 “soft/choppy residential + cost saves offset” to Q3 “residential got worse + pricing pressure picked up,” while C&I backlog commentary has steadily strengthened (helped by Progressive and SPI).
